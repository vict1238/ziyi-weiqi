Output ONLY a square flat top-down seamless-looking light golden kaya wood surface, uniform soft diffuse illumination, very subtle fine vertical wood grain, low contrast, warm pale honey #e9bf7e / #e2b574. ## brand.png

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-11-05 for this project. The reference was the small two-stone wooden-disc brand mark in the user-supplied interface design. The design reference itself is not distributed.

The original tool output is preserved at the default generated-image location. This project copy is the unmodified 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG returned by the tool; the prompt requested a square 1024px canvas, but the built-in output uses its default size. The full wooden disc and both stones are visible, with genuine alpha transparency outside the mark. Four corner alpha values are zero. Application icon sizes are derived by normal asset resizing when packaging.

Prompt:
> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a standalone premium desktop Go (Baduk / Weiqi) app brand icon, raster PNG with a truly transparent background. Create a compact elegant mark of exactly two glossy smooth Go stones resting on a small shallow circular honey-colored wooden disc. A charcoal-black stone sits at the upper-left/back of the disc, and an ivory-white stone sits at the lower-right/front, slightly overlapping the black stone in the visual composition. The light wooden disc has very subtle natural grain, a softly rounded thin rim and a slight three-quarter top-down perspective, so its outline is a soft oval but the entire icon composition stays balanced and nearly square. Warm cream and golden wood palette, soft warm studio light from upper left, gentle dimensional shadows only on the disc and immediately beneath the subject. Refined calm product rendering, clear silhouette legible as a small 24-48px desktop icon. Center the complete intact mark within a square 1024x1024 canvas with approximately 10% transparent breathing room on all sides. No cropping of the disc or either stone, no text, no letters, no watermark, no badges, no Go board grid, no extra stones, no background scene, no opaque rectangle, and no painted checkerboard. Preserve genuine alpha transparency outside the object. This is the brand mark for a warm ivory and brown Windows Go app, with the same understated feeling as a little circular wood base with two black and white stones in a desktop interface design.
